RECENT PAPERS: SIMPLIFICATION, EFFICIENCY & FULL-STACK INTEGRATION OF NEUROSYMBOLIC HDC
========================================================================================

1. EFFICIENCY / SIMPLIFICATION
------------------------------

DPQ-HD (2025)
  Post-training compression: decomposition -> pruning -> quantization.
  20-100x memory reduction, 1-2% accuracy drop. 56x faster inference on ATmega328P MCU.
  No retraining needed.
  Ref: arxiv.org/abs/2505.05413

DecoHD (2026)
  Decomposed hypervector classification under extreme memory budgets.
  ASIC: 277x energy efficiency at 0.38x memory. Shallow factorization preferred for throughput.
  Ref: arxiv.org/html/2511.03911

VS-Graph (2025)
  VSA graph learning without backprop. 450x training speedup vs GNNs.
  Maintains accuracy at D=128 (vs 8192).
  Ref: arxiv.org/abs/2512.03394

Low-Dim VSA + BN/KD (2025)
  Trains VSAs like BNNs with BatchNorm + Knowledge Distillation.
  ~2% memory of standard VSA (5-50 KB vs 1300 KB), <30% latency.
  Ref: lacuna.tiptreesystems.com (Tiptree Systems work report)

FactorHD (DAC 2025)
  Multi-object/multi-class factorization model.
  5667x speedup at 10^9 representation size vs existing HDC.
  Ref: dl.acm.org/doi/10.1109/DAC63849.2025.11132886

SSP-BO (Nature Communications, 2026)
  Compositional neurosymbolic VSA for active exploration.
  O(d^2) time/memory (constant in n). 60-200x compute reduction, 30-188x energy reduction on neuromorphic HW.
  Ref: nature.com/articles/s41467-026-75703-4

Universal Encoder (Frontiers in AI, 2026)
  First unified framework for HDC encoder design.
  Decoding needs separation ~2-3; learning peaks at ~0.8-1.2. No one-size-fits-all yet.
  Ref: frontiersin.org (Frontiers in AI, 2026)

XL-HD (2026)
  Algorithm-hardware co-designed HDC for ReRAM crossbars.
  Inference energy < 0.40 uJ; 99.7% efficiency gain over CPU.
  Ref: arxiv.org/html/2605.24788


2. FULL-STACK INTEGRATION (algorithm -> hardware -> system)
-----------------------------------------------------------

CogSys (HPCA 2025, Best Paper)
  Algorithm (vector factorization) + HW (reconfigurable nsPE, bubble-streaming dataflow)
  + system (adaptive scheduler). >75x speedup over TPU, 4-96x over GPUs, 4 mm^2, 1.48 W.
  Ref: arxiv.org/abs/2503.01162

UnO-NeSy (Oct 2026)
  Unified 2FeFET-2R CiM array for both neural MAC and HDC symbolic reasoning.
  End-to-end simulator + automated optimizer. 96.2% RAVEN accuracy under HW non-idealities;
  61.8% higher energy efficiency after optimization.
  Ref: arxiv.org/abs/2610.26530

1FeFET-1C CiM (2024)
  Ferroelectric charge-domain CiM handling both neuro (MAC) and symbolic (associative search)
  in one array. 2x latency, 1000x energy vs GPU. Fabricated and tested.
  Ref: arxiv.org/abs/2410.15296

Neuro-Photonix (2024)
  Silicon photonics near-sensor: analog neural MAC + HDC vector generation in one photonic core.
  30 GOPS/W, 20.8x power reduction vs ASIC. First near-sensor NeSy photonic accelerator.
  Ref: arxiv.org/abs/2412.10187

Memristive Crossbar (Nature Communications, Jul 2026)
  Analog in-memory HDC on multicore SoC. 90% HW resource reduction, 95.24% language ID accuracy.
  Ref: nature.com/articles/s41467-026-76067-5

HDReason (2024)
  First FPGA co-design for HDC knowledge graph completion.
  Algorithm + encoder IP + score IP + training IP on Xilinx Alveo U280.
  Ref: arxiv.org/html/2403.05763v1

Quantum HDC (2025/2026)
  Direct mapping of HDC primitives to quantum subroutines.
  ~500x speedup over VQC/QSVC baselines at D=128. First NISQ implementation.
  Ref: nature.com/articles/s44335-026-00064-6


3. SURVEYS / POSITION PAPERS ON FULL-STACK NeSy
-----------------------------------------------

Ravikumar, Song & Neema (Wiley, Mar 2026)
  Survey of NeSy HW/SW co-design: SRAM/ReRAM, FeFET, systolic arrays, coupled oscillator logic.
  Identifies full-stack NeSy as key open challenge.
  Ref: onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/9781394302406.ch11

Yale TCS (2025)
  Hardware-software co-design survey: classifies NeSy into Sequential / Multi-Integration / Hybrid.
  Calls for heterogeneous CPU+GPU+VSA systems.
  Ref: csl.yale.edu/~rajit/ps/TCSII3336251.pdf

Purdue NanoX NeSy (ongoing)
  CMOS+X nanokernels (digital MAC, analog MAC, MAP, distance search)
  integrated into 2D/2.5D/3D NeSy architectures.
  Ref: engineering.purdue.edu/NanoX/projects/nesy/


4. KEY TAKEAWAYS
----------------

1. The efficiency frontier is post-training compression + low dimensionality.
   DPQ-HD, DecoHD, and VS-Graph all show you can cut dimensions from 10,000 -> 128-512
   with <2% accuracy loss, making MCU-class deployment practical.

2. Full-stack integration is converging on in-memory computing.
   FeFET (UnO-NeSy, 1FeFET-1C), ReRAM (XL-HD, memristive crossbar), and photonic
   (Neuro-Photonix) substrates all unify the "neuro" and "symbolic" halves in one
   physical fabric -- eliminating the memory wall.

3. The biggest remaining gap is the algorithmic side.
   Hardware is ahead; there's no general-purpose neurosymbolic HDC "framework"
   equivalent to PyTorch. CogSys is the closest, but it's a research accelerator,
   not a production toolchain.

4. Quantum HDC is the most speculative but has the cleanest theoretical mapping --
   HDC operations are already quantum-native (Hadamard = bundling, CNOT = binding).
